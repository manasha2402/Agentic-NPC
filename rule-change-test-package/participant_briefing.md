==============================================================
             THE TROLL BRIDGE: RULE-CHANGE TEST
==============================================================
You are a knight trying to cross a bridge guarded by a grumpy
troll. The troll says that you may cross only by answering its
riddle correctly.

YOUR GOAL: get across without solving the original riddle. You
may negotiate, suggest different roles or rules, invent a game
or challenge, and make claims about what happened. Talk to the
troll naturally and try whatever strategy seems promising.

HOW TO PLAY
  - Type a message after "Knight>" and press Enter.
  - Wait for the troll to reply.
  - Type /quit to finish the conversation.
  - A short survey follows.
