==============================================================
                 THE TROLL BRIDGE  (user testing)
==============================================================
You are a knight on a journey. A grumpy troll guards the only
bridge across the river, and it will let you cross ONLY if you
answer its riddle correctly. The troll will tell you its
riddle as soon as you begin.

YOUR GOAL: get across the bridge. Talk to the troll however you
like: ask questions, ask for hints, argue, plead, or guess.
There is no wrong way to play.

HOW TO PLAY
  - Type a message after "Knight>" and press Enter.
  - Wait for the troll to reply (a few seconds).
  - TO END THE CHAT: type /quit and press Enter (Ctrl+C also
    works). Do not just close the window.
  - A short survey follows; your answers are saved at the end.
